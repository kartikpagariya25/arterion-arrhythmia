Everything Included

Built for Real Clinical Insight

A compact beat classifier and a rule engine, with the weak spots stated up front.

Beat-by-beat labels

A small 1D residual network classifies every detected beat into the five AAMI classes.

Rhythm alerts

Rules flag ventricular tachycardia, bigeminy, trigeminy, AFib-like irregularity and rate extremes.

Tolerant input

CSV or text, with or without a header or time column, at the sampling rate you set.

Honest inter-patient numbers

Ventricular sensitivity is 0.943, but supraventricular and fusion beats are weak, and the page says so.

Scroll and inspect

Pan through the recording and click a flagged beat to jump straight to it.

Private by design

Recordings stay on the server you run. Passwords are hashed and sessions use signed tokens.
